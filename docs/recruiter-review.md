# Recruiter-facing review and redesign requirements

## Assessment of the current presentation

The current design makes a broad personal-brand impression before providing enough evidence to assess Acha for a software engineering role. The visual polish does not compensate for generic copy, repetitive sections, or limited technical detail.

The name and full-stack role should be explicit and prominent. Engineering contributions should appear early. A CTO title should be accompanied by the actual hands-on scope so readers can understand the role without assuming team size or organisational scale. Budgetflow should remain clearly labelled as business analysis and UAT, rather than presented as software personally engineered by Acha.

## Independent content requirements for the next visual direction

### First screen

- Name: Acha Solomon Ona.
- Target role: Full-Stack Software Engineer.
- Supporting description: hands-on development, architecture decisions, code review, and software quality.
- Current context: CTO at Vetandpay; contract business analyst at Lightgate.
- Concrete evidence: restored transaction and escrow functionality and built financial tracking capabilities at Vetandpay.
- Stack: PHP/Laravel, JavaScript, Python; React, Node.js/NestJS, and MySQL supported by the existing CV and user account. Do not imply every technology was used on every project.
- Direct actions: view engineering work and download the current CV, with contact readily accessible.
- Portrait supports identification without displacing the engineering evidence.

### Work evidence

Use the project name, actual role, individual contribution, relevant stack or delivery discipline, and a clear result. Conceptual workflow diagrams must not stand in for evidence of implementation. Avoid fabricated screenshots, code, architecture diagrams, statistics, or testimonials.

Vetandpay is the strongest currently documented engineering example. Budgetflow supports requirements and stakeholder delivery experience. Its case study must identify Lightgate as the contracting organisation and the commission as the project client.

Private work can be credible without a public demo. The main missing evidence is specific, non-confidential technical reasoning: the nature of a defect, the approach chosen, the implementation contribution, and how the result was validated. Do not invent those details to fill a layout.

### Career and technical depth

Show the QA-to-programmer progression at Algoteam followed by current responsibilities, with verified dates. Avoid deriving a total years figure from overlapping or unverified periods. Provide skills with relevant work context rather than ratings, logo walls, or mastery claims.

### Conversion and navigation

Keep the CV, work, and contact one straightforward step away. Shorten the homepage by removing repeated positioning, outcomes, and capabilities blocks where they repeat the same point. Replace broad promotional headings with descriptive headings.

## Selected visual direction

The user selected “Established engineer — polished, confident, corporate.” The copper/cream personal-brand direction was rejected. The current implementation uses white, navy, and blue, a direct name/role introduction, a prominent CV download, and an evidence panel with a compact portrait. Work cards distinguish software engineering from business analysis and UAT.

## Evidence boundaries

- Forum and Shedam remain excluded.
- No quantified 70% performance claim without timings and a measurement basis.
- No assumption that a public landing page is a working system demo.
- No inference of code ownership, team size, compliance, customer counts, or revenue from a company website.
- No unconfirmed frontend or backend implementation role attributed to Budgetflow.
